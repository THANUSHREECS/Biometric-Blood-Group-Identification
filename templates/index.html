Combined Methodological Evaluation Union Estimate
50.50% (51 / 101)
Transparent Evaluation Note:
The combined 50.50% result is calculated by combining out-of-fold predictions for 80 development participants with predictions for 21 participants in the locked test set. It is a methodological combination of these two evaluation groups, not a new independent 101-participant test. The development and locked-test results are reported separately to preserve evaluation transparency.
Development Out-of-Fold Evaluation Cross-Validation
53.75% (43 / 80)
Cross-Validation Benchmark:
80 development participants (240 scans) evaluated strictly via 5-fold StratifiedGroupKFold out-of-fold cross-validation. Each participant was evaluated exclusively by fold models that did not train on that individual. Always-O+ baseline: 36.25% (29/80). Macro F1: 0.4772.
Separate Locked-Test Evaluation Held-Out Benchmark
38.10% (8 / 21)
Held-Out Generalization Reality:
21 strictly held-out test participants (63 scans). Evaluated once on the frozen 5-fold ensemble. Exactly matches the Always-O+ majority baseline (8/21, 38.10%, permutation test p=0.5817). B+ recall: 0.00% (0/6), AB+ recall: 0.00% (0/2).
🔬 Scientific Summary for Faculty Evaluation: Standard optical sensors (500 DPI) capture surface epidermal minutiae, whereas ABO blood antigens are chemical glycoproteins on erythrocyte membranes. Historical literature claiming >85% accuracy suffered from subject leakage across random image splits. When evaluated under strict participant-level isolation, genuine generalization is 38.10% (matching the majority baseline). The deployed system functions as an honest scientific research prototype.

Central Preprocessor

Exact C5 Pipeline: Alpha white compositing -> MFS100 foreground crop -> CLAHE ridge enhancement -> Lanczos 112×112 resize.

E2 Ensemble Inference

Frozen DenseNet121 feature extraction + 3-scan feature concatenation + Dense(512) classification head.
