## 1. Executive Summary & Purpose

This audit establishes the rigorous data isolation and participant-level grouping protocols for **EXP-06B: Supervised Contrastive Loss Weight Ablation**. 

Biometric fingerprint data inherently contains cross-scan correlation across fingers and acquisitions from the same individual. Any contamination across folds or cohorts invalidates the generalization assessment.

This audit programmatically verifies:
1. Strict inclusion of exactly the 80 approved development participants (240 scans).
2. Zero data leakage: strict 100% quarantine of the 21-participant locked test cohort and 26-participant hospital cohort.
3. Perfect participant collocation across all 5 folds: zero participant cross-contamination between training and validation splits.

### 3.2 Leakage & Collocation Guarantees
1. **Zero Scan Leaks:** Every single participant's 3 scans (`scan_01.bmp`, `scan_02.bmp`, `scan_03.bmp`) are assigned strictly to the exact same fold. No participant's scans are split across training and validation.
2. **Mutual Exclusivity:** The validation participant sets across Folds 1 to 5 are strictly disjoint:
   $$\text{Val}_i \cap \text{Val}_j = \emptyset \quad \forall i \neq j$$
3. **Complete Coverage:** The union of validation participant sets equals the complete 80-participant development cohort:
   $$\bigcup_{i=1}^5 \text{Val}_i = \text{Dev Cohort} \quad (|\text{Dev}| = 80)$$
