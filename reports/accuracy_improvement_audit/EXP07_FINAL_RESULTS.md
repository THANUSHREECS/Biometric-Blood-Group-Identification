### Core Scientific Findings:
1. **The Spatial Resolution Hypothesis Is Empirically Rejected:** Preserving optical friction ridge spatial fidelity at $224 \times 224$ (increasing sampling from $\approx 2.67\text{ px/cycle}$ to $\approx 5.33\text{ px/cycle}$) **did not improve** classification performance. Out-of-fold balanced accuracy dropped from 28.61% to 26.15% ($p = 0.6225$).
2. **Performance Remains Within the Random Permutation Null Distribution:** Both $112 \times 112$ ($p = 0.216$) and $224 \times 224$ ($p = 0.396$) operate fully within the null distribution generated by permuting participant labels.
3. **Severe Majority-Class Bias Persists:** Neither resolution could classify AB+ (0% recall), with both models collapsing predominantly onto the two largest classes (B+ and O+).
4. **Generalization Bottleneck Confirmed:** Destructive downsampling was *not* the primary cause of model failure. The failure is driven by the absence of mutual information between optical surface ridges and ABO antigens, compounded by self-reported label noise.

## 2. Experiment Configuration & Verification

All experimental parameters were locked prior to execution and remained 100% unaltered:

- **Cohort Partitioning:** Exactly 80 development participants (240 scans).
- **Target Comparison Variable:** Spatial input resolution ($112 \times 112$ vs $224 \times 224$).
- **Preprocessing:** Alpha composite $\to$ Platen foreground crop $\to$ Lanczos resize $\to$ raw float32 $[0, 255]$ (Round 14 unshifted distribution; no CLAHE, no bilateral filtering).
- **Backbone:** DenseNet-121 (pretrained ImageNet), strictly frozen ($7,037,504$ non-trainable parameters).
- **Fusion:** Three-scan feature concatenation ($3 \times 1024 = 3072$-dimensional participant embedding).
- **Classification Head:** Linear `Dense(512, relu)` $\to$ `Dropout(0.5)` $\to$ `Dense(4, softmax)`.
- **Projection Head:** Linear `Dense(128, activation=None)` for Supervised Contrastive Loss ($\tau = 0.05, \lambda = 0.50$).
- **Trainable Parameters:** Exactly **1,968,772 across 6 tensors** in both conditions (**0.00% parameter confound**).
- **Optimization:** Adam with `CosineDecay` schedule (initial $\text{lr} = 10^{-3}$, decay $= 10^{-4}$), batch size 16.
- **Training Duration:** Exactly 80 epochs per fold under the Fixed-Duration regime.
- **Total Completed Runs:** 5 outer folds for Arm A + 5 outer folds for Arm B = 10 complete training runs. Zero aborted or failed folds.

## 6. Paired Statistical Hypothesis Testing

Because both models were evaluated on the **exact same 80 participants across the exact same 5 folds**, paired statistical tests were executed:

### A. Paired McNemar's Exact Test
- **Discordant Participant Contingency Table:**
  - $b$ (Arm A correct, Arm B incorrect): **12 participants**
  - $c$ (Arm A incorrect, Arm B correct): **10 participants**
  - Total discordant predictions: $b + c = 22$
  - Concordant correct predictions ($A \text{ correct} \land B \text{ correct}$): **17 participants**
  - Concordant incorrect predictions ($A \text{ incorrect} \land B \text{ incorrect}$): **41 participants**
- **Exact Binomial $p$-Value:**
  $$p = 0.8318$$
- **Conclusion:** There is zero statistically significant difference in classification accuracy between $112 \times 112$ and $224 \times 224$.

### B. Paired Permutation Test ($10,000$ Resamples)
- **Observed Difference in Balanced Accuracy:**
  $$\Delta_{\text{bal\_acc}} = \text{BalAcc}_{224} - \text{BalAcc}_{112} = 26.15\% - 28.61\% = -2.45\%$$
- **Two-Sided Paired Permutation $p$-Value:**
  $$p = 0.6225$$
- **Conclusion:** The difference of $-2.45\%$ between the two resolutions is completely consistent with random chance.

### C. Participant-Level Permutation Null Distributions ($1,000$ Permutations)
- **Arm A ($112 \times 112$):**
  - Empirical Balanced Accuracy: $28.61\%$ (Null Mean: $25.11\% \pm 3.92\%$) $\implies \mathbf{p = 0.216}$
  - Empirical Macro F1: $0.2800$ (Null Mean: $0.2481 \pm 0.0401$) $\implies \mathbf{p = 0.237}$
- **Arm B ($224 \times 224$):**
  - Empirical Balanced Accuracy: $26.15\%$ (Null Mean: $25.11\% \pm 3.91\%$) $\implies \mathbf{p = 0.396}$
  - Empirical Macro F1: $0.2506$ (Null Mean: $0.2459 \pm 0.0396$) $\implies \mathbf{p = 0.437}$
- **Conclusion:** Neither resolution achieves statistical separation from random label shuffling.

## 7. Sources of Uncertainty & Limitations

1. **Self-Reported Label Noise:** The primary 101-participant cohort has 100% self-reported labels without medical serological confirmation. Known epidemiological misclassification rates (10%–25%) severely degrade supervised training signals.
2. **Small Cohort Size ($N=80$):** In an 80-participant cohort, each participant represents $1.25\%$ of accuracy. The 95% Wilson confidence intervals span $>20\%$ (Arm A: $[26.6\%, 47.2\%]$), leaving high statistical uncertainty.
3. **Sensor-Specific Representation:** The Mantra MFS100 optical platen introduces varying contact pressure, moisture levels, and optical reflections that DenseNet-121 (pretrained on ImageNet dogs, cats, and vehicles) cannot separate from biological dermatoglyphic ridge structures.

## 8. Strategic Roadmap & Next Scientific Steps

### What We Have Conclusively Established:
1. Historical high accuracy (86%) was caused by lack of participant separation and background shortcuts on the legacy dataset.
2. Historical development accuracy (53.75%) was an artifact of callback selection leakage on outer held-out folds.
3. When evaluated under strict, leak-free fixed-duration cross-validation, the true development accuracy of DenseNet-121 three-scan concatenation is **$33.75\% \text{ to } 36.25\%$**, which is completely aligned with random chance ($p > 0.20$) and matches the locked test result (33.33%).
4. Spatial input downsampling ($112 \times 112$) was **not** the cause of failure. Doubling resolution to $224 \times 224$ did not improve accuracy ($p = 0.8318$).

Rather than sweeping further deep learning architectures or resolutions, the definitive next experiment should test whether **classical dermatoglyphic biometric features** possess any statistical correlation with blood groups:

   - Minutiae count (ridge endings and bifurcations via skeletonization).
2. Train an $L_2$-regularized Logistic Regression or Random Forest classifier on Dev-80.
3. **Scientific Value:** This directly tests the published anthropological literature, providing an interpretable, biological benchmark that deep neural networks cannot obscure.
